# Controls & How-To

## Dial a gate from the Destiny console

1. Use the console to open its destination database.
2. Click a destination row. Use **Previous** and **Next** when needed.
3. Click **Select** to start dialing.
4. During dialing, the center control becomes **Cancel Dial**.
5. During a connection, it becomes **Disconnect**.

## Dial with the handheld

1. Hold the Destiny Kino Remote.
2. Sneak-use it until the tooltip reports **Universe Gate Database** mode.
3. Use it normally to open the address list.
4. Select an address and press **Select**.

The remote locates a usable nearby Universe gate. A Destiny console does not need to be nearby.

## Open Kino controls

1. Hold the Destiny Kino Remote.
2. Sneak-use it until the tooltip reports **Kino Control** mode, then use it normally.
3. The upper-right button opens gate controls; the button below opens Kino controls.
4. The bottom-right button returns home or leaves live video.

## Deploy and recover a Kino

| Action | Result |
|:--|:--|
| **Deploy** with a Kino in inventory | Consumes the item and creates a hovering Kino in front of the player |
| **Deploy** while one already exists | Changes the existing Kino to Follow mode |
| **Follow** | Makes the Kino trail behind its owner |
| **Hold** | Stops following and maintains position |
| **Recall** | Removes the Kino and returns the item |

## Fly the Kino

The screen provides **Left**, **Forward**, **Right**, **Up**, **Back**, and **Down** controls.

When no GUI is open, hold the remote in either hand and use:

| Key | Action |
|:--|:--|
| Up Arrow | Move forward |
| Down Arrow | Move backward |
| Left Arrow | Move left and turn left |
| Right Arrow | Move right and turn right |

The arrow keys also work during live video. Horizontal movement follows nearby terrain height. Use the screen's **Up** and **Down** buttons for manual altitude changes.

## Use live video

1. Deploy a Kino and open the Kino page.
2. Press **Video**.
3. Move the mouse to pan through 360 degrees and look up or down.
4. Use the arrow keys to fly while watching.
5. Press `Esc` or the bottom-right back button to leave video.

Leaving video restores the camera to its forward home direction.

## Scan a destination

Press **Scan**. Telemetry appears in chat with the dimension, atmosphere classification, temperature index, altitude, and biome.

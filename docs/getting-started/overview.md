# Stargate Journey: Destiny Technology

Bring Destiny's gate room and shipboard technology into **Stargate Journey** on Forge 1.20.1 or NeoForge 1.21.1. Build and operate the Universe gate console, explore with Kinos, and opt in to an extensive Destiny ship with working systems.

This guide covers Destiny Technology **1.0.0-beta.2**. Features and requirements can differ between loaders; install the release matching your game.

## What the addon includes

- A functional Destiny dialing console for seven- and nine-symbol addresses.
- Lore-inspired bearing lights, floor chevron lighting, electrical effects, and steam discharge.
- A handheld controller that can dial gates without a nearby console.
- Deployable Kino probes with flight controls, terrain-following movement, atmosphere scanning, Stargate travel, and a live remote view.
- Multiplayer configuration for limiting the number of active Kinos.
- An optional packaged Destiny ship, Kino Dispenser, construction blocks, sliding airlocks, and door controls.
- Ship life support, environmental suits, air scrubbers, water and oxygen storage, and a control interface.
- An eight-stop elevator and a shuttle with a Destiny–Eden route.
- Destiny-route destinations, Water Planet hazards, and Jungle Planet exploration with Squiggler predators.

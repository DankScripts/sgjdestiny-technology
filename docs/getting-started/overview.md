# Stargate Journey: Destiny Technology

Bring Destiny's gate room technology into **Stargate Journey** on Forge 1.20.1. Build and operate the Universe gate console, carry the handheld remote, deploy Kino probes, watch their live camera feed, and reproduce Destiny's dialing effects.

## What the addon includes

- A functional Destiny dialing console for seven- and nine-symbol addresses.
- Lore-inspired bearing lights, floor chevron lighting, electrical effects, and steam discharge.
- A handheld controller that can dial gates without a nearby console.
- Deployable Kino probes with flight controls, terrain-following movement, atmosphere scanning, Stargate travel, and a live remote view.
- Multiplayer configuration for limiting the number of active Kinos.

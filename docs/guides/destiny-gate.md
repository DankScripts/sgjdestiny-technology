# Destiny Gate System

## Normal seven-symbol dialing

During a normal seven-symbol address, the Destiny Bearing flashes when each chevron locks and goes dark between locks. After the final chevron succeeds, the bearing stays illuminated, the floor chevron illuminates, and the wormhole opens.

## Nine-symbol dialing

The programmed nine-symbol sequence represents dialing Destiny. During this sequence, the bearing remains powered through dialing, flashes intensely on each lock, and produces the Destiny electrical discharge effect. The final lock illuminates the floor chevron before the wormhole forms.

{: .lore }
The separate behavior matches the visual language used aboard Destiny: ordinary local dialing uses distinct bearing lock pulses, while the special nine-chevron sequence carries a sustained, unstable energy effect.

## Floor chevron

Use a **Universe Stargate Chevron** from Stargate Journey as the floor marker. Place it horizontally in front of the gate, along the line between the gate and console. It activates only after a successful final seventh or ninth lock, remains on for the connection, and clears when the gate returns to idle.

## Gate shutdown and steam

After a Destiny-controlled gate finishes closing, nearby Destiny Floor Vents wait briefly and then emit sustained angled steam with a continuous pressure hiss. The vents use an exposed polished Naquadah-Copper body when the relevant Stargate Journey block is available while retaining their recessed grates.

## Kino hold-open behavior

When a Kino travels through an outgoing connection from Destiny, the gate stays open so its feed and telemetry remain useful. The connection closes when the console or handheld orders a disconnect, or after a player follows through as supported by the gate lifecycle.

# Destiny Gate System

## Normal seven-symbol dialing

During a normal seven-symbol address, the Destiny Bearing flashes when each chevron locks and goes dark between locks. Once the connection is established, the bearing stays illuminated and the floor chevron is lit. On Forge, bearing effects require SGJourney Deco; NeoForge also supports the addon's fallback bearing.

## Nine-symbol dialing

The programmed nine-symbol sequence represents dialing Destiny. During dialing, the bearing pulses on each lock, as with a normal dial. When a compatible bearing is present, the electrical discharge begins after the seventh lock and continues through the later locks and an established outgoing connection. The final lock illuminates the floor chevron.

{: .lore }
The special nine-chevron sequence adds an unstable electrical effect to the ordinary bearing lock pulses.

## Floor chevron

Use a **Universe Stargate Chevron** from Stargate Journey as the floor marker. Place it horizontally in front of the gate, along the line between the gate and console. It activates at the final seventh or ninth lock or when a connection is established, remains on for the connection, and clears when the gate returns to idle.

## Gate shutdown and steam

After a Destiny-controlled gate finishes closing, nearby Destiny Floor Vents wait briefly and then emit sustained angled steam with a continuous pressure hiss. The vents use an exposed polished Naquadah-Copper body when the relevant Stargate Journey block is available while retaining their recessed grates.

## Kino hold-open behavior

When a Kino travels through an outgoing connection from Destiny, the gate stays open so its feed and telemetry remain useful. The connection closes when the console or handheld orders a disconnect, or after a player follows through as supported by the gate lifecycle.

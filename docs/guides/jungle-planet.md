# Jungle Planet

The Jungle Planet recreates the humid alien world reached after Destiny's expedition visited the ice planet. It contains dense jungle growth, bamboo, vines, caves, native wildlife, and an overgrown Universe Stargate pedestal.

## Dialing

Open the handheld gate page and select **Jungle Planet** from the known destinations. Its Kaliem-region address uses six destination symbols plus the point of origin, forming a normal seven-chevron address.

## Squigglers

Squigglers are fast, low-profile pack predators inspired by the venomous creatures encountered by the expedition. They spawn naturally in groups of two to five and inflict a short poison effect when they bite.

Bring armor, food, and a Kino. A Kino can scout the vegetation and report the planet's atmosphere and biome before the player leaves the gate area.

{: .warning }
Squigglers are difficult to spot beneath dense plants and can close distance quickly.
